# Recorded audio credits and editing disclosure

The runtime bank uses recordings only. No old oscillator/noise-generated effects are included. Sources were trimmed, converted to mono PCM, gently edge-faded and attenuated where necessary. No artificial reverb was baked into these assets. Spatial reverberation is calculated in game.

## Salamander Grand Piano v3 — Alexander Holm
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Recorded acoustic grand piano, 48 kHz / 24 bit. Selected velocity-8 notes converted from FLAC to mono PCM and trimmed to nine seconds. The player-piano pieces (Beethoven Bagatelle WoO 59, public domain, and three original melodies) are performed from these recorded samples by the bank builder: each note is the nearest recorded sample resampled by at most 1.5 semitones, deliberately mistuned per key with beating second strings, rubato and damper release. They are sample performances, not recordings of a continuous live performance. In game the keys, radios, jukeboxes and gramophones add slight pitch wow. Original mapping by kinwie.

## Limits
Several related mechanisms share recordings (for example, pump and laundry motor). Organ/toy/detuned instruments currently use the same recorded acoustic grand samples; no electronic substitute is synthesized. Ambient steam and generic wind/room-tone sources are intentionally silent. Human listening and real device profiling are still required to judge final realism.
